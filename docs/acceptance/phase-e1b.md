## Sources (checked 2026-11-23)
- **District shapes:** the Census Bureau's cartographic boundary file `cb_2024_us_cd119_500k.zip` (`https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/`), 7,045,981 bytes, SHA-256 `2b4238ae…b031a` (pinned in the Makefile). 441 districts: 435 seats, the six at-large states coded `00`, DC and five territories coded `98`. Terms, from its metadata: "free to use in a product or publication, however acknowledgement must be given to the U.S. Census Bureau as the source"; display at 1:500,000 or smaller; no access constraints. Fetched once by `make geo`, not at run time. Each state page with a district map credits it in its sources.
- **State shapes:** `us-atlas` 3.0.1 (npm, ISC licence), `states-albers-10m.json`, derived from Census boundaries; pinned by the lockfile and named, with its SHA-256, in the generated files' headers.
- **Build-time packages:** the owner's go-ahead, 2026-11-23, for `us-atlas`, `shapefile`, `d3-geo`, `topojson-server`, `topojson-simplify`, `topojson-client`, pinned exactly in `scripts/geo/`.

## Open questions found during the phase
- PRD §6.2 asks for a vacant seat's "reason and the date of any special election". The roster source does not give either for a vacancy. Until a source that does is added, the page shows the seat as vacant, and, where the stored roster has it, who last held it and when the source stopped listing them.
- The plan's colour modes include the governor's party (E3b) and the next election (E6a); E1b has the Senate and House delegations only, as planned.
- Dense urban districts (Los Angeles, New York City) are too small to tell apart on a state's map at page size; the list beside the map is the way to them (ADR 0006). District numbers are drawn only on states with 20 districts or fewer.
- On a phone the national map's labels are too small to read; the list below it is the control there, as web-conventions intends.

## Review findings not fixed, and why
Reviewer pass 1, 2026-11-23: two blocking, six should-fix, five nits; all fixed.
- **Blocking:** B1 `make geo` ran `npm ci` in a container with the worktree mounted (AGENTS.md: never mount host paths); it is now the Dockerfile's `geo` stage, installing with `--ignore-scripts`, and regenerating this way gave byte-identical shapes. B2 from 2027-01-03 the 120th's members would have been drawn on the 119th's lines; the API now says which Congress the sitting House belongs to (from the roster, not the clock) and the page draws the old lines plain, with no member on them, and says why (tested on both sides; looked at with a database copy moved to 2027; gate G2 updated).
- **Should-fix:** the national map's shapes are a lazy chunk (entry bundle 227 → 121 KB; the bundle check fails if they return); `/api/states` cites the apportionment and the state lines; a vacant Senate seat names its last holder; list links are 44 px touch targets; the owner's phone is a pending manual line; a seat emptied twice names the later leaver (tested, checked to fail with the order reversed).
- **Nits:** an unused import; the shapes test compares against `geo.DistrictShapesCongress`; a tie between two groups with one party code leads with that code (no one-colour stripe); assertion messages; `make map-check` runs Chrome with its sandbox.

Reviewer pass 2, 2026-11-23: two blocking, three should-fix, four nits; all fixed.
- **Blocking:** the January caption said the new Congress's lines were "not yet published", which is false (the states published them; only the Census Bureau's national file is missing); it now says only that the members sit in another Congress and are not drawn on these lines. A tie was decided over (party, caucus) groups while the map colours by party code, so Independents' and third parties' seats were split before the comparison and the major parties' were not (PRD §3); the API now sends the tie's two codes (`stripes`) and the page draws them; the counting rule itself was corrected again in pass 3 (below).
- **Should-fix:** a seat emptied, refilled by a special election and empty again named the first leaver; `LastHolders` now takes each office's latest term and names its holder only if we saw them leave (tested); the Senate last holder's link is a 44 px touch target; `make map-check` re-run on this version and the January view looked at in dark and at 360 px (below).
- **Nits:** `make geo` builds into a temporary directory and swaps it in; the state lines have their own checked date; "since 1935" for the January 3 start; a duplicate line in the bundle check. The us-atlas source link was checked: it resolves (HTTP 200).

Reviewer pass 3, 2026-11-23: one blocking, no should-fix, no nits; fixed.
- **Blocking:** pass 2's fix totalled seats per colour code, so a Green's and a Libertarian's seats were added together under O while each major party was counted alone (PRD §3, the same arithmetic). Seats are now totalled per party (by name, whatever the caucus, so two Independents caucusing apart are still one party), and never across parties; the parties tied for the most seats give the lead, or one code when they all share it, or a tie striped with their first two codes in a fixed letter order.
